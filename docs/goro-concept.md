goro is a minimalist, browser-based physics puzzle and tower-stacking game 

A complete breakdown of how the game works and what makes it engaging includes:
Core Gameplay Mechanics

    The Crane: A swinging crane continuously swings back and forth across the screen, holding a structural floor slab.  
    highrise.lol

    Timing & Drops: You must click, tap, or press space at the exact right moment to drop the slab onto your growing tower.

    Physics & Balance: The game features a physics engine. If you drop a block off-center, your tower shifts, leans, or can destabilize entirely. If a slab slides off or the stack tilts too far, it's game over.

Progression & Milestones

    Real-World Scale: As you successfully stack floors, the game tracks your vertical height in real time (measured in meters and total floors).

    Landmark Comparisons: Reaching certain altitude milestones compares your tower's height to famous real-world structures, monuments, and geological landmarks.

Features & Layout

    Streaks & Leaderboards: It tracks your current building streak and features daily and all-time leaderboards to see how your stacking skills match up against other players globally.

    No-Frills Design: It runs entirely in-flight inside any web browser with zero loading screens, downloads, or sign-up walls required.
